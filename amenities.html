The house

Amenities

The hotel stays small on purpose. These are the things guests use every day.

Breakfast

Served from 7:30 to 10:30 in the lakeside room. The table holds rye, porridge, seasonal berries, and cheese from a farm nearby.

Coffee is refilled without asking. On Sundays the kitchen adds cardamom buns.

Sauna

The wood-fired sauna is heated every evening from 17:00 to 21:00. Towels are at the changing room door.

In summer, guests cool off from the jetty. In winter, there is a hole kept open in the ice when it is safe.

Lake access

Two rowing boats are free for guests and returned to the same mooring. Life jackets are in the boathouse.

A marked path follows the shore for about two kilometres and returns through birch woods to the car park.

Parking

The courtyard holds twelve cars and two spaces wide enough for a van. Parking is included with the room.

An electric charger is available; ask at the desk so it can be switched on for your bay.
