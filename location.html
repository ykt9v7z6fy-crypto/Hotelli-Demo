Location

Hotel Aurora sits on the eastern shore of Savonlinna, a short ride from the castle and the railway station.

How to arrive

By train, travel to Savonlinna station and take a taxi for about ten minutes. Tell the driver Rantatie 12.

By car from Helsinki, follow highway 5 toward Mikkeli and then route 14 into Savonlinna. The drive is about four and a half hours. The hotel car park is behind the main house.

Savonlinna Airport is fifteen minutes by car. There is no hotel shuttle; a taxi from the airport desk is the simplest way in.
